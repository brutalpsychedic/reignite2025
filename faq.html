Frequently Asked Questions

What is Reignite?

Reignite is a multidisciplinary research and innovation competition hosted by BRAC University Research for Development Club (BUReD), inspired by the July 2024 civic uprising in Bangladesh.

Who can participate?

Any student enrolled in a university, college, or higher secondary institution in Bangladesh can participate.

Can I join more than one segment?

Yes, you may join multiple segments as long as you register separately and manage the deliverables for each.

Do I need to pay a registration fee?

No. Reignite is a free event with no cost to participants.

How do I register?

Each segment page contains a "Register Now" button that redirects you to a Google Form specific to that segment.

What’s the last date to register?

All registration deadlines are mentioned on the Timeline page. Please refer to that page for updates.
