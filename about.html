What is ReIgnite?

ReIgnite is the flagship national event organized by the BRAC University Research for Development Club (BUReD) , designed to empower students to research, reimagine, and revolutionize solutions for pressing national issues. This year’s theme draws inspiration from the historic July 2024 uprising in Bangladesh, focusing on youth-driven innovation, policy critique, and democratic reform through a research lens.

The event is interdisciplinary in nature, welcoming participants from all backgrounds to present, design, or creatively express research-based ideas tackling real-world socio-political, economic, environmental, or technological challenges.

🧩 Segments at a Glance :

~ Core Segments: Poster Presentation, On-Stage Pitch, and Research Paper Submission — the heart of Reignite's academic rigor.

~ Innovative Segments: Meme Battle, Abstract Contest, Stage Podcast, and Infographic & Research Art Contest — where creativity meets critical thinking.

🎯 Why Participate?

Reignite encourages students to not only engage in intellectual exploration but also to step outside the bounds of conventional research presentations. Participants collaborate in teams or individually, build networks, get mentored by professionals, and showcase ideas to a panel of judges from academia, industry, and civil society.

📆 When & Where?

Held on-campus at BRAC University, the event spans over several days filled with workshops, exhibitions, interactive segments, and pitching rounds — creating a festival of research for changemakers.

This is more than a competition — it’s a space to question, collaborate, and contribute. Come fuel the fire. Let’s ReIgnite Bangladesh’s future — together.
