Meme Battle

Mix humor and intellect to create viral-worthy research memes.

Objective

Encourage creativity and critical thinking by letting participants express research and current issues through humor and visuals.

Participation

Individual or duo participation is allowed. Memes must be original and relevant to research, academia, or event themes.

Format

Participants submit 1–3 image-based memes in JPEG or PNG. Captions must be in English and not exceed 20 words.

Evaluation Criteria

Judged on wit, originality, message clarity, and relevance. Offensive or plagiarized content will be disqualified.

Role in Event

Meme Battle adds a playful break to an academic event—while still challenging participants to distill research into simple, viral messages.
