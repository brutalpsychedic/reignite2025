Infographic

Tell a data-driven story using compelling visuals and concise text.

Objective

Push students to combine research findings with design thinking, producing infographics that are both informative and engaging.

Participation

Individuals or pairs may participate. Backgrounds in research, design, or journalism are welcome but not required.

Format

Submit a single-page infographic (JPEG/PNG, A3 size) summarizing a key issue or research project. Visual clarity and data accuracy are essential.

Evaluation Criteria

Entries will be judged on layout, clarity, creativity, relevance, and accuracy of data. Language must be accessible and concise.

Role in Event

This segment bridges research with the power of visual storytelling—ideal for online and offline exhibition and public engagement.
